## Provenance statement (verbatim intent, from PRD §6.3)
Everything is synthetic and generated by the team, including voice samples (recorded by team
members, who consent and are the only people audible) and screenshots (captured from our own
simulators, never a real system). No scraped data. No real ticket exports. No customer names,
even as jokes. Maintain `data/PROVENANCE.md` per dataset: generator script, generation date,
schema rationale, and — where a schema imitates a real product's API shape — a note that the
shape is modelled on publicly documented field structures and contains no real/customer/proprietary data.
